# Simple Starter Cast Choices

Choose **one complete cast: A, B or C**. Each cast has only:

- 1 playable hero
- 1 main villain
- 1 sidekick
- 1 civilian confidant
- 1 political candidate

You can also mix categories, for example: `Hero A, Villain B, Sidekick A, Confidant C, Candidate A`.

> These are creative development choices. The selected final names will still need focused trade mark and marketplace clearance before public release.

# Choice A — Rescue and Rebuilding

## Hero: Goldseam — Amara Saye

A public-building conservator who sees structural damage as glowing lines. She can draw stress out of walls, bridges and roads, storing it inside ceramic tokens before releasing it safely.

- **Strength:** protecting civilians and repairing environments
- **Weakness:** overloaded ceramic tokens crack and become dangerous
- **Appearance:** cream work jacket, oxblood trousers and glazed ceramic plates with amber seams
- **Personality:** compassionate, exacting and unimpressed by heroic showmanship

## Villain: Breakbulk — Rafi Calder

A former freight handler who can separate manufactured objects into the smallest components in which they were originally shipped. A vehicle becomes crates and parts; a prefabricated wall becomes stacked panels.

- **Goal:** dismantle the corporations that automated his community out of work
- **Weakness:** cannot affect living beings, raw materials or handmade objects
- **Appearance:** freight-yard workwear, numbered cargo tags and heavy rigging gloves—not powered armour

## Sidekick: Pinlight — Sol Reyes

A young projectionist who leaves tiny lights visible only from one chosen angle.

- Marks evacuation routes
- Creates simple visual decoys
- Highlights evidence without disturbing it
- Has almost no direct combat power

## Confidant: Mara Voss

The funny, direct owner of **Second Spin**, an all-night neighbourhood laundrette. She hears what is happening across the city from night workers and displaced residents, but refuses to become the hero’s unpaid intelligence service.

## Political candidate: Ada Merren

A former flood-response coordinator campaigning for transparent emergency agreements. She supports heroes only when civilians retain command authority and every operation receives a public damage report.

## What this cast tests

- Environmental rescue mechanics
- Civilian evacuation
- Destructible and repairable locations
- Public trust and rebuilding
- A sympathetic villain rather than a world-destroying tyrant

### Why I recommend Choice A

This is the easiest cast for an initial vertical slice. Goldseam’s powers interact directly with damaged environments, Breakbulk creates those environmental problems, Pinlight communicates objectives clearly, and the two civilians support the trust/rebuilding systems.

# Choice B — Investigation and Truth

## Hero: Blind Index — Inez Kaur

A municipal archivist who perceives things deliberately omitted from reports, maps, photographs and databases—but only while her eyes are closed.

- **Strength:** finding hidden evidence, routes and victims
- **Weakness:** while using her power, she cannot perceive anything properly documented
- **Appearance:** saffron tabard, dark safety lenses, white archive gloves and coloured index tabs
- **Personality:** curious, mischievous and furious about institutional erasure

## Villain: Velvet Proof — Elodie Crane

Anything Elodie says while touching velvet feels like a genuine memory to listeners, even when they intellectually know it is false.

- **Goal:** replace messy public truth with emotionally satisfying stories
- **Weakness:** impersonal physical evidence that nobody narrates is resistant to her power
- **Appearance:** elegant ordinary clothing with small velvet accessories; no illusionist costume or mask

## Sidekick: Backscatter — Hani Cole

A young radio hobbyist who reads the shape of a room from reflected wireless signals.

- Maps unseen spaces and moving objects
- Detects active electronics
- Becomes visible to every network being scanned
- Must choose between secrecy and useful information

## Confidant: Jon Bellamy

A neighbourhood radio host and obsessive record collector. He understands public sentiment through callers rather than chasing secret identities or manufacturing scandals.

## Political candidate: Gideon Shaw

A civil-liberties barrister who distrusts masks, privately gathered evidence and unaccountable powers. He may oppose the hero while being morally correct about due process.

## What this cast tests

- Detective-board and evidence systems
- Dialogue and credibility choices
- Hidden rooms and alternate routes
- Media reputation
- Courtroom or public-inquiry gameplay

# Choice C — Movement and Consequences

## Hero: Crossfade — Ilyas Noor

A night-tram controller who can connect two places sharing the same repeating rhythm: rail joints, blinking signs, machinery or played music.

- **Strength:** rapid traversal and civilian extraction
- **Weakness:** both rhythms must remain synchronised; disruption can strand travellers briefly between locations
- **Appearance:** ink-blue tram overshirt, copper-thread cuffs and a curved wooden tuning frame
- **Personality:** sociable, restless and more loyal to night workers than institutions

## Villain: Tomorrow’s Bailiff — Edda Noll

Edda brings an object’s maintenance consequences forward by exactly one year. Rust, worn brakes, flat batteries and weakened seals arrive instantly.

- **Goal:** expose the human cost of deferred public maintenance
- **Weakness:** well-maintained or newly repaired objects give her almost nothing to accelerate
- **Appearance:** municipal inspection coat, dated maintenance labels and folding test instruments

## Sidekick: Mortarbloom — Tessa Imani

A horticulture student who accelerates helpful lichens living in old brick mortar.

- Cushions falls
- Reveals structural cracks
- Separates masonry safely
- Cannot work on sterile new construction

## Confidant: Celeste Anwar

A ferry engineer and union representative. She provides harbour access and technical help but has her own campaigns, shifts and political obligations.

## Political candidate: Linh Orrell

A cooperative-platform founder campaigning for residents to own the city’s data and resonance infrastructure instead of government departments or private companies.

## What this cast tests

- City traversal
- Timed rescue routes
- Infrastructure condition
- Preventive maintenance
- District ownership and political choices

# Simple reply form

Reply with either:

```text
I choose Cast A.
```

or:

```text
Hero: A
Villain: B
Sidekick: A
Confidant: C
Candidate: A
```

You may also request one small change, such as:

```text
Cast A, but give me three alternative names for Goldseam.
```

After you choose, the next step is to create only those five characters in a small test data pack. The rest of the roster can remain disabled until the first gameplay loop works.
