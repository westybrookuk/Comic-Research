# ⚡ Vanguard: The Greatest Hero — Superhero RPG & Career Simulator

**Vanguard: The Greatest Hero** is a modern, deep superhero career management simulation and tactical role-playing game built with React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, and the Web Audio API.

Inspired by the deep career simulation mechanics of classic text-based hero simulators (such as *Comic Book Hero: The Greatest Cape*), **Vanguard** modernizes and elevates the experience with vibrant comic book aesthetics, procedural sound synthesis, tactical turn-based grid combat, dynamic comic issue generation, moral alignment paths, sidekick mentorship, civilian dating & romance, city hall politics, and a complete in-game Universe Editor.

---

## 🌟 Key Features & Systems

### 1. 🦸 Four Dynamic Game Modes
- **Custom Hero Studio**: Full 5-step character creator. Choose your hero moniker, civilian alias, gender, motto, emblem, costume colors, power kit draft, civilian day job, and allocate 12 core attribute points.
- **Cinematic Origin Stories**: Experience branching narrative incidents (*"The Quantum Incident at Apex Labs"*, *"Shadows of the Blood Alley"*, *"The Fallen Cosmic Fragment"*) with pivotal moral choices.
- **Established Legends**: Step directly into the boots of iconic Metro Nova heroes like **Solaris**, **Nocturne**, **Titanix**, **Circuit Breaker**, or **Dr. Mythos**.
- **Danger Room Combat Simulator**: Sparring matchmaker to pit any hero against any supervillain to test builds and power synergies.

### 2. ⚖️ Moral Code & Alignment Spectrum
- **Three Core Philosophies**:
  - **Golden Age Paragon (+40 to +100)**: Non-lethal justice, +30% Public Approval, +25% Comic Royalties, inspiring presence.
  - **Urban Dark Vigilante (-39 to +39)**: Ruthless efficiency in shadows, +20% Critical Strike Multiplier, +25% Clue Discovery.
  - **Ruthless Anti-Hero (-40 to -100)**: Uncompromising lethal force, +30% Raw Strike DMG, +50% Intimidation, heightened police suspicion.
- **Moral Fate Choices in Combat**: Choose whether to **Subdue with Restraint**, **Interrogate Mercilessly**, or **Execute Lethal Judgment** upon defeated bosses.
- **Philosophical Vows**: Swear sacred vows (*Vow of Absolute Sanctuary*, *Vow of Shadow Reckoning*, *Vow of Total Elimination*) to unlock powerful passive perks.

### 3. 🎓 Sidekick Mentorship, Legacy & Mantle Succession (Era 2)
- **Recruit Prodigies**: Scout young heroes like **Volt Spark**, **Cyber Hawk**, **Nightblade**, or **Aura**.
- **Danger Room Drills**: Train apprentices in Combat, Reflexes, Gadgetry, or Morality.
- **Double-Team Synergy Moves**: Summon active sidekicks in combat for devastating joint attacks.
- **Passing the Torch (Era 2)**: Stage an epic ceremony passing your cowl, secret base, and legendary relics (*Aegis Solar Crest*, *Shadow Weave Cloak*, *Quantum HUD Visor*, *Adamantine Gauntlets*) to your protégé. The successor initiates Era 2 with a dynamic Comic Issue #1 relaunch and generational pedigree bonuses!

### 4. 🎙️ Live Press Conferences, Media Scandals & PR Spin Hub
- **Press Podium Q&A**: Step up to the microphone before investigative journalists from *The Daily Chronicle*, *Channel 6 Action News*, and *Civic Watch Tribune*.
- **Response Stances**: Answer tough questions using **Humble Defender**, **Intimidating Enforcer**, **Charming Celebrity**, or **Transparent Partner** stances affecting Public Approval, Suspicion, and Media Ratings.
- **PR Campaigns**: Host Metropolis Charity Galas or retain top civilian publicists to spin breaking scandals.

### 5. 🌌 Multiverse Incursions & Parallel Universe Variants (Endgame / NG+)
- **Tachyon Incursion Rifts**: Open cosmic gateways to confront parallel dimensional boss variants:
  - **Earth-Omega: Cyber-Dystopia** (*Emperor Solaris Rex*)
  - **Earth-Nether: Inverted Void** (*Grand Inquisitor Dr. Mythos*)
  - **Earth-Steam: Chrono-Empire** (*Chronos Prime Automaton*)
- **Prestige Passives & NG+ Ascension**: Harvest celestial Multiverse Shards to purchase passives (*Tachyon Hyper-Reflexes*, *Dimensional Weave Armor*, *Cosmic Resonance*) and initiate New Game Plus with +50% XP multipliers and inherited heirlooms.

### 4. ❤️ Civilian Social Life, Romance & Milestones
- **Dating Scenarios**: Schedule romantic dates with civilian love interests across Metro Nova (Helios Observatory Stargazing, Artisan Cafés, Midnight Jazz Lounges).
- **Milestones Album**: Unlock memory badges (Downtown Loft Lease, Senior Career Promotion, First Skyline Date, Mayor's Key).
- **Work-Life Balance**: Manage workplace suspicion, mental stress, take mental health days, or put in overtime shifts at your civilian day job.

### 5. 🏛️ City Hall Politics, Elections & Civic Reconstruction
- **Mayoral Election Races**: Endorse mayoral candidates (**Thomas Sterling**, **Victor Vance Sr.**, or **Evelyn Cross-Sterling**), host joint campaign rallies, and influence municipal policies.
- **District Reconstruction Hub**: Fund repairs and contribute hero labor to restore battle-damaged landmarks across Downtown, the Docklands, Neon District, and the Gothic Quarter.

### 6. 📻 Comic Soundboard & Audio Engine
- **Multi-Pack Web Audio Synthesizer**: Switch between **'90s Animated Synth**, **'60s TV Vintage**, **Modern Cinematic**, and **8-Bit Arcade** sound profiles.
- **12 Interactive Launchpads**: Trigger procedural sound effects (POW strikes, heavy smashes, laser blasts, force barriers, mystic heals, level-up arpeggios, sirens, and fanfare).
- **Battle Cry Voice Synthesizer**: Speak hero catchphrases and custom text-to-speech quips in real time.

### 7. 🏙️ Living City Simulation: Metro Nova & Narrative Event Deck
- **6 Tactical Districts**: Downtown Metropolis, The Iron Docklands, Neon District, Gothic Quarter & Asylum, Aero-Tech Park, and Subterranean Underbelly.
- **Procedural Narrative Event Deck**: Dynamic choice-driven story cards encounterable during patrols featuring D20 dice checks, stat prerequisites, and moral dilemmas (*Runaway Mag-Lev Monorail, Bank Vault Hostage Siege, Leaking Dark Matter Bio-Canister, Corrupt Alderman Ledger, Nether-Rift at St. Jude, Escaped Sentient AI*).
- **Villain Nemesis & Masterplans**: Villains plot doom conspiracies (Singularity Rift, Sub-City Conflagration, Neural Grid Lockdown) and adapt reactive defenses against past defeat tactics.
- **Mega-Crisis Crossovers**: Multi-stage coalition operations against extraterrestrial armadas and rogue AI swarms with comic event rewards.

### 8. ⚔️ Tactical Turn-Based Combat Engine
- **Action Point (AP) & Super Meter**: Manage energy to unleash Melee strikes, Ranged energy blasts, Shields, and devastating Ultimate Moves.
- **Status Effects**: Inflict and resist **Burn, Stun, Bleed, Freeze, Weakened, Empowered, and Shielded** conditions.
- **Comic Visuals & FX**: Floating comic badges ("POW!", "WHAM!", "ZAP!", "KRAK!"), screen shake, floating numbers, and dynamic sound cues.

### 9. 🗞️ Dynamic Comic Book Publishing
- **Weekly In-Game Issues**: Real-time comic book issue generator with custom cover art, letter grades (S+, S, A, B, C, D), sales circulation numbers, reader fan mail, and royalty payouts.

### 10. 🛠️ Complete In-Game Universe Editor
- Create, customize, and balance Heroes, Villains, Superpowers, and Districts.
- Export universe databases as JSON files and import community-created universes.

# 3. Build for production
