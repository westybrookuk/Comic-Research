1. CONFIDANT AWAKENING LORE & SYSTEM RULES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Can Civilian Confidants (like Chloe Rivera) become superheroes?
YES! In Vanguard's universe, civilian confidants follow two narrative paths:

1. THE GROUNDED CIVILIAN ANCHOR (Default Path):
   - Keeping Chloe Rivera as your civilian best friend is vital for gameplay balance:
     she runs Roaster's Haven Café, anchors your hero's humanity, listens to your
     double-life struggles, and provides the passive "-50% Stress & -15% Suspicion"
     recovery perk that keeps your hero from burning out or having their identity leaked.

2. THE SUPERHERO AWAKENING ARC (Story Milestone / Succession / Editor):
   - Through milestone achievements, sidekick promotions, or the Universe Editor,
     confidants can awaken their own heroic mantle:
     * CHLOE RIVERA -> "PRISM" (The Luminescent Aegis): Hard-light crystal shieldcaster.
     * DR. SARAH STERLING -> "LIFELINE" (The Bio-Aegis Medic): Exosuit combat surgeon.
     * LIAM VANCE -> "BLACKLINE" (The Shadow Sleuth): Undercover stealth hacker.
     * CAPT. ELENA RAMIREZ -> "PRECINCT-ONE" (The Law Enforcer): Tactical police mech pilot.

   - Defeated villains who escape prison remember your favorite attacks, build counter-tech
     (e.g., if you spam Fire, they return with Heat-Absorbing Cryo Armor), and ambush you
     during civilian dates or press conferences!

3. INTERACTIVE BASE OF OPERATIONS (HQ ROOM BUILDER):
   - Upgradeable 2D floorplan rooms for your Secret Sanctuary:
     * Trophy Room (displays defeated boss masks & stolen relics)
     * Danger Room / Holo-Simulator (train stats & test combos)
     * Crime Supercomputer (boosts district patrol detection)
     * Medbay & Cryo-Tanks (speeds injury recovery)

   - Interactive visual-novel style dating and social encounters with Chloe or Dr. Sterling.
   - If a bank robbery sirens go off during dessert, choose between:
     [A] Excuse yourself to the restroom (Hero +20, Romance -10, Suspicion +15)

     [C] Reveal secret identity right then and there!

   - When deploying with a sidekick or alliance member, trigger cinematic Dual-Techs
     (e.g., Solaris + Volt Spark = "Solar Supernova Rush", Nocturne + Dr. Mythos = "Shadow Rift Ambush").
