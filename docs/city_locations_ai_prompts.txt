1. APEX TOWER PENTHOUSE SPIRE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
District: Downtown Financial Hub
Category: Landmark & Corporate Headquarters
Tagline: Gleaming 110-story skyscraper piercing the stormclouds with a neon holographic beacon.
Description: The pinnacle of corporate wealth and technological ambition in Metro City.
The helipad offers breathtaking 360-degree vistas of the illuminated skyline, with
floor-to-ceiling reinforced smart glass and corporate laser security grids.

Tactical Environmental Hazards:
- 120 mph Gale-force winds (Knockback chance)
- Glass penthouse shatter zone (Shrapnel damage)
- Active corporate surveillance laser grids

Lighting & Mood: High-contrast midnight noir, neon teal and amber corporate holograms, rain-slick reflections.

Master AI Generation Prompt:
Comic book concept art of Apex Tower penthouse helipad at midnight, towering above Metro City, dense rainstorm, hyper-detailed cyberpunk skyscraper architecture, glowing neon cyan and gold corporate holograms, cinematic wide-angle hero shot, dynamic atmospheric lighting, 8k comic illustration, Jack Kirby meets Moebius style --ar 16:9 --style raw

Negative Prompt: blurry, low quality, photorealistic, messy line art, dull colors
